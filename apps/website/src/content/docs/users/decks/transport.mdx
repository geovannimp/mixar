The transport is the pair of keys under the performance panel:

| Key | Label |
| --- | --- |
| Play / pause | **Play** while stopped, **Pause** while playing |
| Cue | **Cue** |

Both keys use the same deck-wide enable rule: they are dead (and drawn at half opacity) when the engine is not running, while a track is loading, or when the deck holds no track.

## Play and pause

**Play** starts playback from the playhead and becomes **Pause**; pressing it again stops where the playhead is. Nothing is re-cued when you pause and play again.

## Cue

The **Cue** key has two behaviours decided by how long you hold it:

- **Tap**: release under 180 ms sets the cue point at the playhead. With **Q** on, the point snaps to the beat grid.
- **Hold**: keep it down past 180 ms and the deck auditions from the cue point, playing forwards. Releasing returns the deck to where it was before the hold, in the state it was in. Playing stays playing, paused stays paused.

A deck with no cue point yet auditions from its current position on the first hold.

The key lights up while you press it, and stays lit through an audition.

## Vinyl brake

Off by default. **Settings → Deck → Vinyl brake pause** is the card, its switch is **Brake on pause**, and its description reads "Pause spins the deck down like a turntable instead of cutting instantly."

With it on, pausing ramps the platter to a stop instead of muting:

| Setting | Range | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stop time** | 0.05–3.0 s, 0.05 s steps | `0.50 s` |

The hint under it: "How long the platter takes to stop after pause."

The brake is suppressed while your finger is on the jog and starts when you lift off a paused platter. Pressing play or loading a track cancels a brake in progress, so the ramp never rides into new audio.

## Eject and load

Both actions live on the track cover, next to the title, rather than in the header:

- **Empty deck**: click the cover to open the library and pick the track there.
- **Loaded deck**: hovering or focusing the cover grows its eject chip until it fills the cover, so the artwork itself is the unload button. Clicking it clears the deck. The library entry, cues, loops and beat grid stay; you are unloading the player, not the track.
- **Playing deck**: the same click asks **Unload Deck A?** — **Cancel** keeps the track playing, **Unload** clears it.

The eject chip dims while the engine is stopped and while a load is in flight, so it cannot race the deck. [Decks overview](/docs/users/decks#cover-actions) has the full set of cover states.

## Time readouts

The track info shows remaining time as `-m:ss.t` and the total duration as `m:ss.t`. Both read **—** before anything is loaded.

Loops and hot cues move the playhead without touching the transport. See [Loops](/docs/users/decks/loops) and [Hot Cues](/docs/users/performance-pads/hot-cues).
