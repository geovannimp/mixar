Two displays show the same track at different scales.

| Display | Where | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overview strip | Along the bottom of the deck's track info, 36 px tall | The whole track in one bar, plus its loop and cue markers |
| Scrolling lane | The waveform pane at the top of the mixer, one lane per deck | A zoomed window (about 24 seconds at normal pitch) with the playhead pinned in the middle |

With no tracks loaded, the waveform pane reads **Load tracks to see waveforms.**

## Moving the playhead

| Gesture | Where | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Click or tap | Overview strip | Seeks to that point in the track |
| Drag | Scrolling lane | Scrubs; the view follows your finger and the audio follows the drag |

Dragging the lane sends seeks while you move, throttled so a fast drag does not flood the engine, and a final seek on release. Between gestures the playhead eases back onto the engine's position rather than jumping: small drift is corrected gradually, and only a real jump (180 ms or more, or any pause) snaps it.

## Colours

Each band of the spectrum has a fixed colour:

| Band | Colour |
| --- | --- |
| Low | `#FF4830` |
| Mid | `#76E458` |
| High | `#48BCFF` |

**Settings → Waveform → Display** holds the two ways to draw them. **Display mode**:

| Mode | Look |
| --- | --- |
| **RGB** (default) | Low, mid and high mixed into one colour per bar |
| **Filtered** | Three stacked bands, low widest |

The section description reads "RGB mixes low/mid/high into one color. Filtered stacks the three bands."

## Overlays

Drawn on top of the waveform, in the lane and on the overview:

- **Beat lines**, with every fourth beat brighter as the bar line.
- The **active loop** as a filled region, and a pending loop-in as a flagged vertical line.
- **Hot cue** flags at each saved cue point.
- **Saved loop** blocks, dimmer than the active loop.
- The **slip shadow** playhead, when **S** is on and the shadow has drifted away from the audible position.

## The jog

The **Jog** tab holds a platter with a progress ring around its rim and a marker that turns with the audio. Drag anywhere on it to turn the platter.

Dragging holds the platter, so with the default top mode the deck treats it as a hand on the record: turn it while playing and the audio scratches, let go and the motor picks the platter back up with a short ramp. The progress ring tracks how far into the track you are.

How the platter responds is set under **Settings → Deck → Jog wheel**:

| Setting | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Top jog mode** | Vinyl (scratch), Pitch bend, Ignore | Vinyl (scratch) |
| **Outer jog mode** | Vinyl (scratch), Pitch bend, Ignore | Pitch bend |

**Ignore** makes that surface inert. On a paused deck a **Pitch bend** surface scrubs instead: there is no tempo to bend when nothing is playing, so the platter moves the playhead.

The card's description reads "Defaults for top (touch) and outer (freewheel) platter policy." The on-screen platter counts as the top surface, so **Top jog mode** is what it follows.
