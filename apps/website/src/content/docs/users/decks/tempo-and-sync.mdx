Use the tempo column to change a deck's speed on its own, or to make it follow the master. The column holds the pitch fader, its range chip, and the sync key. Key lock sits on the key chip in the track info. The column is the narrow rail on the inner edge of the deck, with no card chrome. A single hairline runs down each side, and the rail spans the full deck height. It meets the waveform pane above and the library divider below.

## Read the column

The top block of the column stacks three things:

| Line | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| BPM | The sounding tempo, two decimals, scaled live by the fader. `—` when the track has no analysed BPM; `000.00` while the track is still loading |
| Pitch | The fader's signed offset, e.g. `+0.00%` |
| Status icon | Crown when this deck is master, closed lock when synced, open lock when not |

That whole block is the sync key: clicking it toggles sync.

## Bend the tempo

1. Drag the vertical fader off its centre notch. Centre is 0%; the ends are plus and minus the current range.
2. Watch the pitch line for the signed offset as the fader moves.

The fader is disabled while sync is active, because a synced deck's tempo is being driven for it.

## Cycle the tempo range

1. Click the chip under the fader. Its tooltip is **Tempo range**: "Pitch fader span. Click to cycle the max ±% range."
2. Click again to step through `±6%` → `±10%` → `±16%` → `±25%` → `±6%`.

The fader stays where it is across the change. A centred fader keeps the track at its own tempo; an off-centre one lands on a new pitch. The range a deck starts with comes from **Settings → Deck → Default tempo range**.

## Sync to the master

1. Crown the deck that should lead with **Set master**, the header key on every other deck.
2. Click the readout block on the deck that should follow for tempo sync, or Shift+click it for beat sync.
3. Click the block again to turn sync off. The tempo stays where it is.

| Gesture | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Click | Tempo sync: "Tempo follows the master. Click again to turn sync off." |
| Shift+click | Beat sync: "Tempo and phase follow the master. Click again to turn sync off." |
| Click while synced | Turns sync off and leaves the tempo where it is |

Tempo sync matches the master's effective BPM. Beat sync does that and also pulls this deck's phase onto the master's beat, clicking the decks into alignment. The status icon shows a closed lock while the deck is synced.

Both need an analysed BPM on both decks. Without one, sync raises "Master deck BPM is required for sync." or "Slave deck BPM is required for sync." Beat sync raises the same message with "beat sync" in place of "sync". Beat sync also respects the deck's **Q** setting when it lands the phase. With quantize off it aligns to the beat without snapping the seek.

## Master

One deck is the tempo reference. Deck A holds it at startup until you crown another deck with **Set master**, the header key on every other deck. The master's own sync key is inert; there is nothing above it to follow. Mixar refuses the attempt with "Master deck cannot sync to itself. Choose the other deck."

## Key lock

The key chip in the track info is also the key lock control.

1. Load a track. The chip is disabled until the engine is running and a track is loaded.
2. Click the key chip to toggle the lock. The tooltip names the state it is in.

| State | Tooltip | Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On | **Key lock on** | "Keeps musical pitch when you change tempo." |
| Off | **Key lock off** | "Pitch follows tempo changes." (vinyl behaviour) |

It is off by default and each deck starts from **Settings → Deck → Default key lock**, whose hint reads "Tempo-only pitch when on (time-stretch). Off = vinyl tempo."

The chip always shows the **sounding** key: the analysed key transposed by the deck's current key shift. The display mode comes from **Settings → Library**. That transposition is display-only and never written back.

## Key shift

The Pads surface's **Shift** mode transposes the whole deck by up to 16 semitones. See [Key Shift](/docs/users/performance-pads/key-shift). It is a separate control from key lock: lock decides whether tempo drags the pitch, shift moves the pitch deliberately.

## What listens to the tempo

Sync, quantize, loops, roll and beat jump all read the deck's BPM and beat phase. A track whose BPM is wrong drags sync, quantize and beat jump down with it, so correct the grid in [Beat grid](/docs/users/decks/beat-grid) first.
