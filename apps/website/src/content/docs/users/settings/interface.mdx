**UI** holds the theme, language, control presentation, and first-run setup cards. **Layout** holds the presets, the deck count, the panels the mixer shows, where the waveform sits, and how the library opens. **Waveform** holds the display mode and the EQ/gain shaping switch.

**Save** writes these preferences. The theme, the accent color, and the layout controls apply without restarting the engine, since they only change how the app paints. **Deck layout** asks for confirmation and restarts it.

## UI → Theme

Card subtitle: "Brightness and the accent color used by brand chrome."

| Control | Type | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Theme** | select | **System default** / **Light** / **Dark** | System default | App brightness source. |
| **Accent color** | swatches | **Teal** / **Blue** / **Violet** / **Orange** / **Pink** | Teal | Hue used by brand chrome. |

**System default** follows the host's light/dark preference. **Light** and **Dark** pin the app theme regardless of the host.

**Accent color** re-tints brand chrome: buttons, selection washes, the waveform playhead, text selection, and the lit mixer signals (SYNC, KY, the headphone cue icon, FX). Deck faders keep their sky/rose identity and the deck **CUE** button stays amber. The chosen hue follows the theme: light mode uses the deeper steps, dark mode the brighter ones.

Saving **Theme** or **Accent color** does not restart the engine.

## UI → Chrome and controls

Card subtitle: "Language, hover tips, and how selects open."

| Control | Type | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Language** | select | **System default** / **English** / **Português (Brasil)** | System default | Language of the app chrome. |
| **Show tooltips** | checkbox | on / off | on | Hover tips on controls. |
| **Select style** | select | **Auto** / **Desktop** / **Mobile** | Auto | How selects and ⋯ menus open. |

- **Auto**: "Platform default — desktop OS uses popovers, phones use dialogs."
- **Desktop**: "Anchored popovers for selects and ⋯ menus."
- **Mobile**: "Open selects and ⋯ menus in a dialog."

**Auto** resolves against the host: on desktop it behaves as **Desktop**, on a phone as **Mobile**. Saving these three controls restarts the engine.

## UI → First-run setup

Card subtitle: "Show the welcome steps again instead of the mixer. Saved with the rest of Settings."

| Control | Type | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Show setup again** | button | — | — | Clears the "setup done" flag in the draft; nothing happens until you Save. |

The button clears the "setup done" flag in the settings draft, and nothing happens until you save. After Save, the app leaves the mixer and shows the setup stages again, exactly as on a fresh install. The controller stage appears only when a supported, mapped controller is detected. The stages are described in [First launch](/docs/users/getting-started#first-launch).

This save does not restart the engine, so audio keeps playing while you are sent back to setup.

## Layout → Presets

Section subtitle: "Which panels the mixer shows, where the waveform sits, and how the library opens." Card subtitle: "Start from a device, then show or hide individual panels."

| Control | Type | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Preset** | select | **Desktop** / **Tablet** / **Mobile** | Desktop | Sets the panel switches below in one go. |

| Preset | Waveform | Mixer strip | FX bar | Library |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Desktop** | on | on | on | docked |
| **Tablet** | on | on | on | drawer |
| **Mobile** | on (middle) | off | on | drawer |

A preset is a shortcut, not a mode: it sets the switches below in one go. Change a switch afterwards and the select follows whatever the panels now are. It reads **Custom** with "No preset matches the visible panels below", or another preset when the result happens to match one. Flipping the drawer on Desktop lands on **Tablet**, and back.

**Mobile** centers a top or bottom waveform between the decks, and either middle position keeps it reading **Mobile**. **Desktop** and **Tablet** lift a centered waveform back to **Top**, because they promise the mixer strip. No preset changes the deck count or the crossfader switch, and saving one does not restart the engine.

The FX bar stays on in every preset. It is the only way into the **Levels** dialog, which holds the master and headphone levels.

## Layout → Deck layout

Card subtitle: "How many decks the mixer shows. Changing it restarts the audio engine and reloads tracks."

| Control | Type | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Decks** | select | **2 decks** / **4 decks** | 2 decks | Deck count the mixer shows. |

Switching to 4 decks stacks A/C on the left and B/D on the right around the shared mixer. Changing it asks **Change deck layout?** with **Restart and apply**. The change restarts the engine and does not restore tracks on decks removed by going back to 2 decks.

This is the one control under **Layout** that is not cosmetic, because the engine is built for the deck count it starts with.

## Layout → Mixer strip

Card subtitle: "Channels, faders, EQ, crossfader, and meters between the decks. Turning it off widens the decks into a single row, or hands the column to the centered waveform."

| Control | Type | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Show mixer strip** | header switch | on / off | on | Draws the shared mixer between the decks. |
| **Crossfader** | checkbox | on / off | on | Shows the crossfader strip and its hairline. |
| **Mixer meters** | select | **None** / **Mono** / **Stereo** | Mono | Deck VU ladders between the volume faders. |

The decks are not a switch: they always stay on screen. **Mixer strip** is the shared mixer between the decks, meaning channels, faders, EQ, and meters. With the switch off the decks widen into the freed space and meet on a single hairline, with the mixer's gutter closed. Deck pads, transport, and the FX bar keep working.

**Crossfader** hides the crossfader strip and its hairline at the foot of the mixer column. It hides the same strip under a **Middle horizontal** or **Middle vertical** waveform. The checkbox stays visible while either foot is on screen, so a mid-centered waveform with the strip off can still hide its crossfader.

**Mixer meters** are the deck VU ladders between the volume faders; the master meter stays in the FX bar. **None** removes the meter lane. **Mono** draws one ladder per deck (max of left/right), and **Stereo** draws separate left and right ladders.

The meters only show while the strip is on screen, which a **Middle horizontal** or **Middle vertical** waveform prevents even with the switch on. The crossfader switch also shows when a middle waveform carries the column in the strip's place.

## Layout → FX bar

Card subtitle: "The shared effect unit and master meter at the foot of the mixer pane. Master and headphone levels open from the meter."

| Control | Type | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Show FX bar** | header switch | on / off | on | Draws the effect unit and master meter at the foot of the mixer pane. |

Hiding the bar also removes the **Levels** dialog opener with the master meter it sits in. Master output level, master cue, headphone level, and the cue/master blend are reached from that meter, and the effect unit cannot replace them. Leave the bar on unless you only need the decks.

The library has no switch here: **Layout → Library drawer** decides whether it is docked or drawn. The waveform has its own card below, because visibility and position belong together.

## Layout → Waveform position

Card subtitle: "Top keeps the waveform above the decks; bottom puts it between the decks and the library. The middle positions replace the center mixer with the lanes. Turn the switch off to hide the waveform."

| Control | Type | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Show waveform** | header switch | on / off | on | Draws the waveform lanes. |
| **Position** | select | **Top** / **Middle horizontal** / **Middle vertical** / **Bottom** | Top | Where the lanes sit. |

**Show waveform** off removes the lanes everywhere and hands the center column back to the mixer; the **Position** select hides with it. The **Mobile** preset turns the lanes back on and picks a middle position.

**Top** is the full-width waveform pane above the deck row, with one horizontal lane per deck. It is the only position with a draggable split. **Bottom** keeps horizontal lanes but moves them into a band between the deck row and the library. That band sits above the FX bar when the library drawer is on.

**Middle horizontal** and **Middle vertical** replace the center mixer column with the lanes themselves, inside the deck row. Horizontal lanes stack; vertical lanes sit one per deck side by side. The vertical lanes run bottom to top, and dragging a lane still scrubs.

The mixer channels (faders, EQ, cue) are not shown while either middle position is selected. The crossfader panel stays at the foot of the column. Switch back to Top or Bottom for the whole mixer. A middle position wins over **Layout → Mixer strip**, which only decides between the mixer and no center column at those two positions.

Changing this only moves chrome: saving does not restart the engine.

## Layout → Library drawer

Card subtitle: "Where the library opens. Drawer mode hides the docked library and opens it over the mixer."

| Control | Type | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Library as a drawer** | header switch | on / off | off | Opens the library over the mixer instead of docking it. |
| **Position** | select | **Left** / **Right** / **Top** / **Bottom** | Bottom | Edge the drawer opens from. |

With the drawer off, the library is docked under the decks. With it on, the docked library is gone and the library opens over the mixer from the edge you picked. **Position** is only shown when the drawer is enabled. The **Tablet** and **Mobile** presets turn the drawer on.

- The drawer starts closed. It opens from its header toggle, and nothing reopens it on launch.
- A dimming scrim covers the mixer behind it. The scrim does not block input, so the mixer stays interactive. That is what lets you drag a track out of the drawer and drop it on a deck while the engine is running. A stopped engine installs no drop target, so the row drags but nothing accepts it.
- A press outside the panel, the header toggle, or loading a track onto a deck closes it.
- The inner edge carries a resize grip; drag it to change how much of the mixer the drawer covers. The size holds for the rest of the session.
- Dragging a track out tucks the panel off its edge so the deck is never covered at drop time; the drag end closes the drawer.
- The drawer sits below the app header, so it never covers the window controls.

## Waveform → Display

Section and card subtitle are the same sentence: "RGB mixes low/mid/high into one color. Filtered stacks the three bands."

| Control | Type | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Display mode** | select | **RGB** / **Filtered** | RGB | How the three bands are drawn. |

RGB blends the low, mid and high bands into one colour; Filtered stacks them as three separate bands. Both draw the same analysed data. Saving the display mode restarts the engine. See [Jog and waveform](/docs/users/decks/jog-and-waveform) for what the display shows.

## Waveform → EQ & gain shaping

Card subtitle: "Scale the lane bars with the channel fader and the deck EQ. Off keeps the waveform still while you move them."

| Control | Type | Values | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **EQ & gain shaping** | switch | on / off | on | Whether the scrolling lane follows the channel fader and the deck EQ. |

On, the lane bars scale with the channel fader and the deck's EQ knobs: killing a band collapses its bars, a boost doubles them, and a flat channel matches the stored peaks. Off, the lane draws the recorded peaks unchanged, so moving the fader or an EQ knob leaves the waveform still. The overview strip never follows the EQ either way. See [Jog and waveform](/docs/users/decks/jog-and-waveform).
