**Settings → Controllers** ("MIDI mappings and connected hardware. Trust a device to auto-enable it on connect after Save.") has two cards: **Mappings** and **MIDI ports**.

## Mappings

Card subtitle: "Stored in app data. Seed copies shipped maps when missing; Update overwrites from the app bundle." The header button is **Update All**.

Each row shows the device's vendor and product as its title. Pills beside it give the mapping id, the device id, the bundle version, and **Attached** while the mapping is live. On the right:

| Control | What it does | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Update available** | Appears when the mapping shipped with this build is newer than the copy in app data. | Label only |
| **Update** | Overwrites the app-data copy from the app bundle. | Acts immediately |
| **Trust** | Toggles trust for the device. | Part of the draft; takes effect on **Save** |
| **Attach** | Toggles the mapping on or off. | Acts immediately |

With no mapping bundles in app data yet, the card reads "No mappings in app data yet."; **Update All** seeds them from the bundle.

**Save** restarts the audio engine when it is running. The mapping actions above run before any save; **Trust** is the only row control that waits for it.

## MIDI ports

Card subtitle: "Detected MIDI inputs and outputs, and the mapping each one matches." Every detected port gets a row with an **IN** or **OUT** chip, the port name, and either the mapping it matched or **No mapping**.

With nothing plugged in, the card reads "No MIDI ports detected."

## Connecting a controller

Plug in a controller Mixar has a mapping for and a toast appears: `<device> connected`, then "Do you want to use this controller?". The prompt has an **Always allow this device** checkbox and **Cancel** / **Enable**.

- **Enable** attaches the mapping now.
- **Always allow this device** plus **Enable** also stores the device id in the trusted list. The next time the device is plugged in, the mapping attaches without asking.
- **Cancel** leaves the port alone. It will not ask again until the device is re-plugged.

**Trust** and **Attach** on each row are the same switches the prompt sets.

## What ships

Only the Pioneer DDJ-400 has a shipped mapping. Adding another controller means authoring a mapping bundle: [MIDI mappings](/docs/developers/midi-mappings).
