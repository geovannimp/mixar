Cueing sends a channel to your headphones instead of the main output. The channel is tapped before its volume fader, summed into a cue bus, and sent to the **Preview bus** output. It stays silent until that bus is enabled.

## Before you start

The preview bus must be on and have a device: **Settings → Audio → Preview bus → Enable preview bus**. Set it up under [Audio setup](/docs/users/getting-started/audio-setup) or in the [Audio settings reference](/docs/users/settings/audio). The audio engine must be running too; the **headphone cue** key is inert while it is stopped.

## Cue a deck

1. Press the **headphone cue** key at the bottom of the deck's volume column. It is the headphones icon, and it lights and stays lit for as long as the deck is cued.
2. Cue a second deck the same way. Several decks can be cued at once and are summed, so you can compare two tracks before bringing either in.
3. Click the master meter to open the **Levels** dialog.
4. Set the headphone level with the top fader of the **Headphone** card.
5. Blend the two sources with the **Cue** / **Mst** fader.

## Verify

The cue icon is lit and you hear the cued decks in the headphones. With the **Cue** / **Mst** fader at the **Cue** end you hear pre-fader listen only. The cued deck's meter shows levels even with its channel fader closed.

## Levels

The **Levels** dialog holds two cards.

**Master**: "Output bus volume and master cue." The fader is the master output level; the headphones icon beside it is **Master cue**, which adds the master mix to the cue bus.

**Headphone**: "Cue volume and Cue/Master blend." The top fader is the headphone level. Below the **Mixing** label, the **Cue** / **Mst** fader (announced as "Cue/Mst") blends the two sources and has a centre notch.

When the preview bus is off, the Headphone card shows an **Enable** button. It jumps straight to **Settings → Audio** with the preview bus fields in view and closes the dialog.

## What the headphones carry

The cue tap sits after the channel's FX insert but before its volume fader:

- Channel fader position does not change what you hear in the headphones.
- The crossfader does not either, because it is applied after the channel faders when the lanes are summed.
- Routing the deck through **FX** does change it, since the tap is downstream of the effect.

The deck meters read from the same tap, which is why a cued deck with a closed fader still shows levels.

## How the blend works

The blend is a crossfade between pre-fader listen and the master tap:

| Blend position | What you hear |
| --- | --- |
| **Cue** end (0) | Pre-fader listen only |
| Middle | Both, mixed |
| **Mst** end (1) | Master tap only |

The master contribution is gated by **Master cue**. With master cue off, the master side of the blend is silence, so the fader only attenuates the cue: at the **Mst** end you hear nothing at all rather than the mix.

## If you hear nothing in the headphones

Start with the two checks that cover most cases: **Enable preview bus** is on in **Settings → Audio → Preview bus**, and a channel is cued, so its headphones icon is lit. [Troubleshooting sound](/docs/users/getting-started/troubleshooting#nothing-in-the-headphones) lists the full headphone path, including the headphone level and the blend position.

For the strip, the crossfader sides and the master meter, see [Mixer overview](/docs/users/mixer); for per-control ranges, [Channels](/docs/users/mixer/channels).
