The mixer screen is where you play: a waveform pane on top, the decks around the central channel strips, the library underneath, and a thin **Beat FX** strip pinned to the bottom. Every deck feeds one channel strip, and the strips are what reach the master output and the headphones.

## Signal flow

A channel crosses its strip in a fixed order: **gain trim → EQ → filter → FX → meter and cue tap → fader**. After the fader the crossfader scales the channel, and the scaled channels reach the master output.

That order decides what the controls do to what you hear:

- The meter and the headphones already include the effect, because the tap sits after FX.
- The channel fader still controls how loud the whole channel is, because it comes after the tap.
- The crossfader scales the effect exactly like the dry signal, because it is applied after the channel faders.
- Cueing a deck does not add it to the master, because the tap feeds a separate bus. See [Cueing](/docs/users/mixer/cueing).
- The FX stage is shared: a deck runs through it only while its header **FX** key is on. See [Beat FX](/docs/users/mixer/beat-fx).

## The screen

Choose a two- or four-deck layout under **Settings → Layout → Deck layout**. Changing the layout restarts the audio engine and reloads tracks on decks that remain active.

Four-deck mode gives the decks more vertical room and splits each stacked pair of decks with a hairline. On short windows, use the main scrollbar or mouse wheel to reach the docked library below the decks.

## Deck cards

Deck identity colors stay consistent across headers, waveforms, tempo controls, mixer channels, and library load actions: **A blue**, **B rose**, **C amber**, and **D violet**.

Each deck card holds the track info, the performance pads, the tempo column, the transport (**Cue** / **Play**), and a row of header buttons:

- **Gain**: opens the deck's pre-fader trim.
- **Master**: makes this deck the tempo master (the crown).
- **Q**: quantize. Snaps cue, loop, hot-cue, roll and beat-jump actions to the beat grid.
- **S**: slip. Keeps a shadow playhead moving under loops and scratch, then catches up on exit.
- **FX**: routes this deck through the shared Beat FX unit; see [Beat FX](/docs/users/mixer/beat-fx).

Loading and ejecting sit on the deck's track cover, not in this row; see [Cover actions](/docs/users/decks#cover-actions).

In compact layouts, scroll the deck header to reach its remaining buttons. Use the mouse wheel or the pads' scrollbar to reach both rows without shrinking them to unusable sizes. Dragging a pad remains a performance gesture.

## Channel strips

Every deck owns a channel in the mixer columns between the decks:

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **3-band EQ** | Low, mid and high, centre = flat |
| **Filter** | One-knob DJ filter, centre = bypass |
| **Fader** | Channel volume |
| **Cue** | Sends the deck to the headphone bus |

Knob ranges, the filter's cutoffs, fader travel, the column layout, and what the meters show: [Channels](/docs/users/mixer/channels).

The whole strip can be taken off screen: **Settings → Layout → Mixer strip**. The decks stay and widen into the freed space; deck transport, pads, and the FX bar keep working.

With a **Middle horizontal** or **Middle vertical** waveform, the lanes already hold the center column. This switch then only changes the layout around them, and returning to **Top** or **Bottom** brings the strip back.

In four-deck mode, the controls run left to right as **EQ/filter A+B → gain/volumes A+B → meters → gain/volumes C+D → EQ/filter C+D**. Each gain knob sits directly above its volume fader.

## Crossfader

Click **A** or **B** beside the crossfader to open that side's deck checklist. Check a deck to assign it to that side; assigning it to the other side moves it rather than routing it to both. Uncheck a deck to set it to **Thru**, so the crossfader no longer affects it. The checklist follows assignments made from hardware too.

In a four-deck layout, the default assignments are A/C to side A and B/D to side B.

Set history uses these assignments when deciding whether a playing deck is audible, including decks assigned to **Thru**.

The crossfader and its hairline can be taken off screen: **Settings → Layout → Mixer strip → Crossfader**. The decks keep their assignments, so hardware and set history still follow them.

## Master output

The **master meter** sits at the left of the FX bar under the library. Click it to open **Levels**, which holds the master output level, the master cue toggle, the headphone level, and the cue/master blend for the headphones. See [Cueing](/docs/users/mixer/cueing) for how the headphone bus is built and what each control does.

## Beat FX

The strip under the library is the shared effect unit: pick an effect, set the beat division, set the level, arm it, and choose which decks feed it. It has its own page: [Beat FX](/docs/users/mixer/beat-fx).
