Select the **Cue** tab and the eight pads become hot cues: eight saved positions in the loaded track that you can jump to instantly.

## Before you start

- A track is loaded on the deck. With no track the pads are disabled: [Overview](/docs/users/performance-pads).

## Save, jump, and delete

1. Select the **Cue** tab.
2. Tap an empty pad to save a hot cue at the playhead.
3. Tap a pad that holds a cue to jump to it and start playback from there.
4. Hold **Shift** and press a pad that holds a cue to delete it.

## Check the result

- A saved pad shows its label and the saved time.
- A deleted pad returns to its numbered, empty state.
- Reloading the track later puts its cues back on the same pads.

## Where cues live

Hot cues are saved **with the track** for tracks in your library. Hot cues belong to the track, not to the deck. Loading a different track shows that track's cues, and empty pads for a track that has none.

If the pads are empty after a load, the track has no cues yet. If a pad holds an unexpected position, check that the deck has the track you meant to load.
