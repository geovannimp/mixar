Select the **Cue** tab and the eight pads become hot cues: eight saved positions in the loaded track that you can jump to instantly.

## Setting a hot cue

**Tap an empty pad** to save a hot cue at the playhead. The pad shows its label and the saved time once set.

If the pad already holds a cue, tapping it instead **jumps to it and starts playback** from there.

## Deleting a hot cue

Hold **Shift** while pressing a pad that holds a cue to **delete** it. The pad returns to its numbered, empty state.

## Storage

Hot cues are saved **with the track** for tracks in your library. Re-load the same track later and its hot cues are on the same pads again.

Hot cues belong to the track, not to the deck: loading a different track shows that track's cues, and empty pads for a track that has none.
