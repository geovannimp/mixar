Every deck has a grid of **eight performance pads**, and the **mode tabs** directly above it decide what those pads do. The pads are not fixed-function buttons. The selected mode turns the same eight squares into hot cues, loop rolls, beat jumps, samples, stem controls, or pitch controls. Each deck keeps its own selected mode, so two decks can show different modes at the same time.

## The grid

- **Eight pads** in two rows: pads **1–4** on top, pads **5–8** on the bottom.
- **Mode tabs** sit directly above the grid. Selecting a tab switches what the pads do.
- **Cue** is the default mode when a deck starts.
- The pads need a **track loaded** on that deck. Until then they are disabled, though you can still change the selected mode.
- **Stems** pads additionally need stems for the loaded track, either an [NI Stem file](/docs/users/stems/native-ni) or [generated](/docs/users/stems/generate) first.

## Choosing a mode

| Mode | What the pads do | Reach for it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cue** | Hot cues: save, jump to, and delete marker points | You want to jump back to a saved position |
| **Roll** | Temporary loops that play while the pad is held | You want a loop that ends the moment you let go |
| **Jump** | Move the playhead forward or back by a set number of beats | You want to skip an intro or pull a drifted track back |
| **Sample** | Trigger samples from a bank of up to eight | You want a vocal stab, drum hit, or acapella over the track |
| **Stems** | Mute or isolate the drums, bass, other, and vocals layers | You want to drop one layer or hear one layer alone |
| **Keys** | Play the root hot cue at a chosen pitch | You want a vocal or stab in key without editing the track |
| **Shift** | Shift the pitch of the whole deck up or down | You want to bend a track into the key of what is next |

Two pad modes found in other DJ software are **not available yet**: **Slicer** and **Stems FX**.

## Modifier keys

Several pad actions use **Shift** while pressing a pad: deleting hot cues and samples, and switching semitone pages in Keys and Shift. Hold **Shift** as you press, exactly as you would with a controller's shift bank.

## Beat-based modes

**Roll** and **Jump** move the playhead relative to the track's beat grid, so both need the track **BPM** to have been analysed. With **Quantize** enabled, a Roll starts and a Jump lands on the beat instead of wherever the playhead happened to be. [Beat grid](/docs/users/decks/beat-grid) covers that analysis.

## What is not a pad mode

**Roll** loops are held and temporary. Latching loops that you set, save per track, and recall sit in the deck's own **Loop** tab: [Loops](/docs/users/decks/loops).

**Shift** pads transpose the deck in semitones, so they meet the deck's key lock and tempo controls. [Tempo and sync](/docs/users/decks/tempo-and-sync) covers key lock and the pitch display; [Decks](/docs/users/decks) is the map of the whole deck card.
