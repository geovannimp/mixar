Select the **Stems** tab and the pads control the separated layers of the loaded track: **Drums**, **Bass**, **Other**, and **Vocals**.

## The pads

The top row mutes and unmutes, the bottom row isolates:

| Pads 1–4 | Pads 5–8 |
| --- | --- |
| `mute` drums | `solo` drums |
| `mute` bass | `solo` bass |
| `mute` other | `solo` other |
| `mute` vocals | `solo` vocals |

**Mute** — press to silence that layer, press again to bring it back. The pad lights while the layer is muted.

**Isolate** — press to hear only that layer with the other three silenced. Press the same pad again to clear the isolation and return to the mix of muted and unmuted layers.

## Where stems come from

There are two ways a track gets stems.

### NI Stem files

Tracks that already ship as an **NI Stem file** (`.stem.mp4`) need no preparation. Add them to your library like any other track, then load one onto a deck — the four layers are read straight out of the file, so the Stems pads are ready as soon as it loads. **Generate stems** does nothing for these: the layers are already in the file.

### Any other track

Every other track can be separated on demand. From the library, open a track's row menu and choose **Generate stems**. Separation runs in the background and does not interrupt playback, so you can keep working while it finishes.

Separation happens once per track and the result is reused afterwards — including in later sessions, where the pads are ready as soon as you load the track.

## Requirements

For a track that has not been separated yet, the Stems pads stay disabled until separation finishes. The track plays normally in the meantime; the pads simply do nothing. NI Stem files are ready on load, as above.
