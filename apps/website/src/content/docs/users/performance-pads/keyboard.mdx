Select the **Keys** tab and the eight pads play a single hot cue at eight different pitches — useful for dropping a vocal or a stab in key over a mix, without editing the track.

## Choosing what to play

Keyboard pads always play from a **root hot cue**. Pick the root with the settings button beside the page bar; the bar shows the cue currently selected. Only cues that are actually set on the track can be chosen, so set one in [Hot Cues](/docs/users/performance-pads/hot-cues) first if the list is empty.

## Playing a pad

**Hold a pad** to play the root hot cue at that pad's pitch. **Release** it to stop and return to the root position; the pitch change is temporary and playback continues as before.

Pressing a pad does not change the tempo — only the pitch. If another pad is still held when you release one, the held pad's pitch stays in effect.

## Pages

The pads cover one octave of semitones at a time, spread over **four pages**, starting on page 2. Use the arrows below the pads to move between pages; the page bar shows the range each page covers.

| Page | Label | Pads 1–8 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +8…+12 | +8, +9, +10, +11, +12, empty, empty, empty |
| 2 (default) | 0…+7 | 0, +1, +2, +3, +4, +5, +6, +7 |
| 3 | -1…-8 | -8, -7, -6, -5, -4, -3, -2, -1 |
| 4 | -9…-12 | empty, empty, empty, empty, -12, -11, -10, -9 |

Blank pads on a page do nothing. Pages wrap from 4 back to 1.

## Shortcuts

Hold **Shift** while pressing:

- **Pads 1–6** — delete that hot cue from the track.
- **Pad 7 / pad 8** — move to the next / previous page.
