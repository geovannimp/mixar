Select the **Sample** tab and the eight pads trigger short pieces of audio — a vocal stab, a drum hit, an acapella — from the deck's sampler, alongside the track that is playing.

## Assigning a sample

**Drag a track from the library onto a pad** to assign it. The pad then shows the sample's name and length. Assigned pads can be dragged again to replace the sample.

## Banks

Samples are organised in **banks** of eight slots. The arrows below the pads move through the available banks, and the bank name is shown between them. The settings button opens the bank settings:

- **Name** — rename the bank.
- **Play mode** — how the pads in this bank trigger:
  - **One-shot** — starts a single, non-looping play. Releasing the pad early cuts it off, so hold until the sample finishes.
  - **Hold** — plays while the pad is held and stops on release.
  - **Loop** — repeats while the pad is held and stops on release.
  - **Default** — use the bank's default play mode (One-shot).

The active play mode is shown next to the bank name whenever it is not the default.

## Clearing a sample

Hold **Shift** while pressing an assigned pad to **clear** the sample, leaving the pad empty.
